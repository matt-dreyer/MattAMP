# MattAMP

Personal Linux desktop utility for Matt. Consolidate DI.FM, podcasts, filtered X reading and workstation monitoring into one small desktop app. One media source at a time. The user approved the Sidecar/stacked UI and 56-pixel bug mode in this conversation.

Must support eight DI.FM station presets, five podcast favorites, visible YouTube playback, audio podcast fallback, volume/playback controls, resume positions, network reachability and throughput, CPU, RAM and both NVIDIA GPUs. Show unavailable readings honestly. DI.FM uses the user's own Premium external-player credentials. No account creation, purchases or credential harvesting.

X reading uses the official Latest search with an editable AI circle, defaulting to bcherny, steipete, karpathy, simonw and chamath, an optional AI-keyword restriction, and replies/reposts excluded. Elon Musk has a separate lane. X controls search matching and availability. Sign-in happens directly on X in an isolated local session that the user can forget; never import browser cookies. Provide a browser fallback. Reading keeps radio/audio podcasts running, pauses hidden YouTube, and preserves the workstation monitor and approved layouts. Hide X in Settings, compact and bug modes. The provider owns its page and sign-in appearance; MattAMP's surrounding controls retain the established design.

49-inch 5120×2160 OLED, dark blue-gray surfaces with muted green accents. Low brightness. Desktop app, not a hosted website. Bug mode keeps radio/audio running and pauses YouTube. Restore prior layout. Persist positions and preferences locally. Original system tools remain available until the replacement is demonstrated.

The user approved an optional subtle live background inside MattAMP. Offer Particle flow by default, Water waves, Starfield, Mandelbrot zoom and Off, with the choice saved locally. Use denser particle/star fields and wave lines. Mandelbrot randomizes six detailed destinations and horizontal/vertical glides, curved approaches or slow spirals, varying direction and depth without immediate repeats or abrupt cuts. Keep GPU work bounded and retain the static fallback. Keep motion slow, dim and unobtrusive, with no interference with controls, playback or provider content. Stop and clear the effect when off, hidden, in bug mode or when reduced motion is requested.

Ambient effects respond gently to the mouse over MattAMP: particle flow bends around the pointer, waves form local ripples, stars shift with depth, and Mandelbrot pans slightly. Responses ease in and out, leave clicks and scrolling to the existing controls, and stop with the background under Off, hidden, Bug mode or reduced motion. Embedded provider surfaces retain their own mouse handling.

Use dark translucent glass for buttons and selects so the live backgrounds remain visible. Keep labels and station artwork opaque, preserve selection/hover/focus states and all existing control behavior, and respect reduced transparency.

AMP Artillery adds quick, untimed last-tank-standing skirmishes against 3–5 bots. Offer Very dumb, Casual, Sharp and Human-ish skill levels; opponents use the same wind/physics and do not preferentially target the player. Include destructible terrain, fall/splash damage, unlimited shells and three five-warhead MIRVs per tank. Aim with sliders or click the sky; use Up/Down arrows or scroll over the battlefield or power slider to adjust power, and press Enter to fire, preserve the last shot’s trail, and show turn, health, wind and score. Battles freeze in other tabs, Settings, compact/bug mode and when hidden; radio/audio continue and hidden YouTube pauses. Game sounds have their own mute control. Brief comic speech bubbles react to the complete volley: boasts or wounded complaints for strong hits/knockouts, heckling for wild misses and self damage. Near misses stay quiet; only one bubble is shown, it never blocks input, and pauses/tab changes clear it. The Game tab uses the full width with compact telemetry underneath.

Keep a local top-ten arcade scoreboard with three-character initials, bot count/skill, result and score, plus lifetime completed battles and wins. Save completed results atomically in arcade-scores.json under Electron userData, independent of the changing loopback origin. Retried saves must not duplicate results; abandoned battles do not score. Award points for enemy damage/knockouts and a victory/remaining-health bonus, scaled by bot count and difficulty. This is a personal local scoreboard, not a competitive online ranking. Metal Slug tank/explosion sprites and Worms 2 firing/impact sounds are installed for the personal build; their original copyrights remain intact. Kenney CC0 fallback sprites and victory/defeat cues remain bundled. Source provenance is in assets/arcade.

Heckles use sarcastic, R-rated trash talk about shots and tanks. A player victory triggers a deliberately extravagant nine-second 3D awards ceremony: a rotating metallic gold tank on a plinth, moving camera and lights, orbital rings, and 2,400 shader-animated confetti pieces. A short original fanfare follows the game sound toggle. Escape or Back to scores dismisses it. Losses and draws do not celebrate, scores save independently, and media playback continues. Stop and dispose GPU/audio resources on dismissal, tab changes, Settings, compact/Bug mode, hiding, or starting another game. Reduced motion uses one static 3D frame; unavailable WebGL shows the accessible victory text.
