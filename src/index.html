How to play & credits

No turn timer. Chaos Off keeps wind fixed for the battle; Mixed and Unhinged change it each turn. MIRVs split at their peak into five smaller warheads. Craters and falls damage tanks—including your own. After round 12, blast damage increases to break stalemates. Bots remember damage for three completed battles and are more likely to target repeat winners—even other bots. Grudges fade; abandoned battles don’t count.
