# AMP Artillery assets

Bundled unchanged from Kenney’s CC0 packs, downloaded from the official pack pages on 2026-10-14. Original license files are included alongside `sources.json`, which maps every shipped image/sound to its original filename and SHA-256 digest.

- [Tanks](https://kenney.nl/assets/tanks): side-view hull, tracks and 12 explosion frames. The game draws its own aimable, player-colored barrels, labels, terrain and projectile trails.
- [Sci-fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds): retro firing cue and crunchy explosion.
- [Digital Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio): MIRV split, victory and defeat cues.

The renderer scales and dims the sprites at draw time. Sounds play quietly with at most four simultaneous effects and stop when the game is hidden or paused. No remote asset fetches are needed during play.
